# Declaration on the use of Artificial Intelligence (AI) systems in the preparation of the thesis

the Claude Code command-line interface, and Codex (OpenAI), for the purpose of
assisting with the implementation, debugging and testing of the research codebase,

layout requirements, and reviewing the manuscript for internal consistency.

accordance with its objectives, presents their own intellectual contribution, and
takes full responsibility for the final form of the thesis.
