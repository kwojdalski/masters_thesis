1. MOTIVATION: What goal (task or research problem) has been set in the thesis? Has its
significance been justified?
The thesis sets a clearly defined research problem: whether a continuous-control deep reinforcement learning              5
agent, conditioned on limit-order-book microstructure features, can extract a risk-adjusted edge at tick
frequency, and what economically bounds that edge. The significance is convincingly argued on two levels -
theoretically, because at tick frequency execution conditions are part of the signal environment rather than an
implementation detail, so the decision problem genuinely differs from bar-based trading; and methodologically,
because the applied RL-trading literature is shown to be weak precisely on cost modelling and evaluation
discipline. The author explicitly frames the study as a controlled simulation rather than a live HFT system and
states from the outset what is not modelled (latency, queue priority, partial fills, market impact), which is the
right way to establish scope.

2. RESEARCH QUESTION OR HYPOTHESIS: Have research questions and/or research hypotheses
been posed in the thesis? If so, which ones? [Please note that the requirements in this matter are
summarized on the first page of the review form.]
Four numbered hypotheses are stated in Section 1.1.2 and carried consistently through the entire work: (H1) a             5
TD3 agent trained on LOB features learns a risk-adjusted edge that survives out of sample; (H2) that edge
depends on the assumed transaction cost; (H3) a broader microstructure-aware state representation improves
out-of-sample performance; (H4) the reward objective shapes the policy and not only its score. Each is designed
as a single-factor comparison, in which scenarios differ only in the quantity under study, which is exactly what
makes the hypotheses testable rather than merely illustrative. The author also frames significance against an
economic threshold rather than against zero, correctly observing that with roughly a million steps per instrument
any statistical test rejects at effect sizes far below a tradable margin.

3. METHOD: What research method was used in the thesis? Does it fit the goal of the thesis? Has it
been applied properly?
   a) choice of method and its fit to the goal of thesis, b) scope and adequacy of use.
a) Choice of method and fit to the goal (5/5). TD3 is a well-motivated choice: the exposure decision is naturally        a) 5
continuous, and Chapter III derives the progression from value-based methods through DDPG and PPO to
TD3's twin-critic and target-smoothing corrections, so the selection is argued rather than assumed. Training           b) 3
DDPG and PPO under an identical configuration is a deliberate and correct design move. It converts H1 from a
claim about one optimiser into a claim about a learnable signal. The evaluation apparatus (buy-and-hold,
TWAP, VWAP and a random-action control, chronological splits, strictly causal online normalisation, one
varying factor per hypothesis) fits the goal well.

b) Scope and adequacy of use (3/5). The implementation itself is careful and well documented, but the empirical
scope is narrow and this materially limits what the apparatus can deliver. The dataset covers only four trading
days (25-27 February 2026 for training, 2 March 2026 split into validation and test), so the agent is never
exposed to a different volatility or spread regime, each configuration is trained on a single seed, and three of the
reported runs failed to train at all, collapsing to a constant saturated position, which leaves H3 and H4
unresolved. In addition, all headline results are produced under frictionless mid-price execution, and the bid/ask
repricing that tests their robustness is applied post hoc to a single instrument without retraining. The author
identifies every one of these limitations explicitly and diagnoses the saturation failure carefully, which is to his
considerable credit, but they nevertheless constrain the adequacy of the empirical application. Against this, the
complete code base is published as an open repository and is treated as an integral part of the work rather than
as supporting material: experiments are specified declaratively, so each hypothesis scenario can be re-run from
its configuration file, which makes the causal normalisation, the chronological splits and the one-varying-factor
design auditable rather than merely asserted.

The thesis makes substantial and correct use of knowledge from the Quantitative Finance programme and                    5

acquired knowledge is deepened, and the linkage across disciplines is genuine.

5. LITERATURE AND DATA: Is the selection of literature and/or empirical material accurate and

   a) selection of literature and/or empirical material, b) usage in the thesis.
a) Selection of literature and empirical material (5/5). The bibliography comprises roughly 90 items and is             a) 5
dominated by current international sources spanning theoretical microstructure, deep reinforcement learning,
and applied RL trading, with attention to reproducibility and evaluation-rigour work. The empirical material is       b) 5
exactly right for the question: Nasdaq MBP-10 (ten-level market-by-price) data obtained through DataBento for
six liquid US equities, which is the granularity actually required to construct the queue, imbalance and book-
curvature features the design relies on. The only reservation concerns the length of the sample rather than its
quality, and is scored under question 3b.

b) Usage in the thesis (5/5). Chapter II is not a survey appended for form's sake. It is structured to deliver the
design decisions of Chapter IV, separating descriptive microstructure variables from tradable signals by an
explicit criterion (available before the action, survives costs, improves out-of-sample performance). Prior
applied RL work is used to structure the treatment of action-space design, transaction-cost modelling and
validation rigour. The data are also used properly: the per-instrument decomposition in Table 5 shows that
cumulative return across instruments largely reflects event frequency rather than skill, and the author correctly
switches to a per-step measure.

6. RESULTS: Has the aim of the thesis been achieved? Have the research hypotheses been
verified? Have research questions been answered? Does the thesis have any mistakes which cast on
the correctness of driven conclusions?
The aim is partly achieved and the verdicts are stated with unusual precision. H1 is supported in a qualified            4
form - all three learners separate cleanly from the random control while remaining indistinguishable from one
another, so a learnable signal is present, but the per-step edge is smaller than a half-spread and reverses sign
when the action path is repriced at the bid/ask. H2 is partially supported: the policies are cost-sensitive but no
break-even fee is identified. H3 and H4 are explicitly reported as unresolved, because in each comparison one
or two runs failed to train. What prevents this from being simply a shortfall is the author's own synthesis: three
separately configured zero-fee runs collapsed to a saturated position under identical architecture and budget,
which locates the binding constraint in training stability rather than in feature or reward design, and this is
presented as the study's most substantial finding. No error was found that casts doubt on the conclusions drawn;
an earlier evaluation-window mismatch in the H1 comparison was detected, re-measured over the full test split
and disclosed in Section 7.2.4.
7. LOGIKA WYWODU: Czy sposób prowadzenia i opisu wywodu jest prawidłowy? Czy zachowana                                 0–5
została logika wywodu? Czy dobrze poprowadzona została narracja?
7. LOGIC OF THESIS: Is the way of conducting and describing arguments correct? Has the logic of
arguments been preserved? Was the narrative well conducted?
The argument is conducted rigorously and the logic is preserved throughout: motivation, microstructure                   5
evidence, RL foundations, agent design, implementation, hypothesis-by-hypothesis results, then a synthesis that
separates what is established from what is not. In my opinion, the narrative discipline is the strongest feature of
the thesis. The author distinguishes carefully between a failure that is an economically rational response to fees
and a failure that is an optimisation artefact. Chapter VII does not retreat into generalities but converts the
limitations into a prioritised research plan.
