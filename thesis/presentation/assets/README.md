# Faculty identity

`wne-en.svg` is the English colour logo from the Faculty of Economic Sciences,
University of Warsaw's [official visual identity page](https://www.wne.uw.edu.pl/wydzial/struktura-wydzialu/dzialy-administracji/sekcja-komunikacji/identyfikacja-wizualna).

- Download: [Polish and English logo pack](https://www.wne.uw.edu.pl/download_file/2269/0).
- Original archive member: `LOGO_WNE_2022/EN/WNE_UW_EN_zwykly/WNE_UW_EN_zwykly.svg`.
- Retrieved: 15 September 2026.
- Only the SVG viewport was tightened to remove the surrounding A4 artboard;
  artwork, proportions, colours, and lettering are unchanged.
- Faculty burgundy: `#AA273D`. Arial is the faculty's documented fallback font.

The logo remains the university/faculty's identity asset; it is not original
thesis artwork. The faculty page sets out the conditions for its use, including
student permission through the Communication Section.

The slide layout follows the white background, burgundy headings, and faculty
footer in the [reference lecture presentation](https://coin.wne.uw.edu.pl/lmatuszczak/materialy/Makroekonomia/WNE/prezentacja1_23_V3.pdf),
using the current English faculty logo.
